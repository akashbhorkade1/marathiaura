Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use MarathiAura, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Email address — when you subscribe to exam update notifications

2. How We Use Your Information

We use minimal, essential cookies only. No third-party advertising cookies. Analytics cookies are anonymized and help us understand which content is most useful.

Our site contains links to official government websites (e.g., mpsc.gov.in, ssc.nic.in). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. However, no method of internet transmission is 100% secure. We recommend not sending sensitive personal information through our contact form.

6. Your Rights

  • You can request deletion of your personal data by emailing contact@marathiaura.in

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, email us at contact@marathiaura.in
